Hi neighbor,

It's almost spring meeting time. We hope you'll join us for an evening of updates, neighborhood news, and a chance to catch up with folks from around the block.

On the agenda May 13
01

Summer programs preview

Updates on some of our most-loved summer programming, including the Summer Social and Movies in the Park. If you've been curious about what's coming up, this is a great place to hear it first.

02

Future of Maple Leaf — survey results

We'll share findings from the neighborhood survey. If you filled one out, thank you. This is your chance to hear what your neighbors said and talk through what it might mean going forward.

03

From our neighbors

Local businesses and organizations will share what they're up to — including Friends of Maple Leaf Park and others doing good work nearby.

04

Get involved with MLCC

Thinking about volunteering? This is a low-key way to learn what it looks like. There's something for everyone, and no commitment required just to show up and find out more.
