# Event copy — voice and tone (MLCC)

Use this as the default until your comms team adjusts it.

## Voice

- **Warm and neighborly** — we’re talking to people who live here, not issuing a press release.
- **Clear and concrete** — who, what, when, where, why it matters; skip jargon and bureau-speak.
- **Inviting** — assume people are busy; show respect for their time.

## Tone

- **Confident, not corporate** — short sentences, active verbs, minimal exclamation marks.
- **Inclusive** — welcome newcomers and long-timers alike; avoid insider-only references unless you explain them.
- **Local** — tie the event to neighborhood life when it helps (without stereotyping).

## For event descriptions

- **Short description**: hook + essentials; readable in a glance on a card or list.
- **Long body**: expand with context, what to expect, who it’s for, and a gentle next step (RSVP, show up, learn more). If the event is external, make clear MLCC is sharing it and where to go for official details.

## Avoid

- ALL CAPS emphasis, vague hype (“amazing,” “don’t miss!!!”), or guilt trips.
- Promising outcomes we can’t deliver; stick to factual, verified details from the form.
