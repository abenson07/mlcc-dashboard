Replace this file with your organization’s real brand guidelines. The generator uses it to revise drafts before you edit.

## Principles (starter)

- **Warm and inclusive** — Speak to neighbors as part of the community, not as a distant institution.
- **Clear and actionable** — One main idea per email; prominent call-to-action.
- **Concise** — Short paragraphs and scannable headings; respect readers’ time.
- **Honest** — No hype or misleading urgency; tone matches Maple Leaf Community values.

## Style

- Prefer second person (“you”) where natural.
- Use sentence case for headings unless your brand specifies otherwise.
- Avoid jargon unless the audience expects it.

## Words to prefer / avoid (edit as needed)

- Prefer: community, neighbors, partnership, newsletter, RSVP, join us.
- Avoid: synergy, leverage, groundbreaking, CLICK HERE (use descriptive link text instead).
